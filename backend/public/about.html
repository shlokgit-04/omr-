About OMR Checker

Create an exam, share a link, and let the app grade every OMR answer sheet for you — instantly and fairly.

OMR Checker replaces the tedious manual marking of multiple-choice answer sheets. A teacher sets up an exam with an answer key; students open the exam link on any phone or laptop, fill their roll number and pick their answers (typing them in, or letting the camera capture feature read them), and the server grades every sheet in the same instant and stores the result.

What it does

  • Exam setup in seconds: name an exam, choose the number of questions (up to 200), and enter the A/B/C/D answer key.
  • One shared link per exam: students submit without any account; the answer key never reaches them.
  • Instant server-side grading: scores and percentages are computed when the submission lands, not by trusting the client.
  • Results dashboard: class averages, per-student results, and a one-click CSV export with per-question answer columns.
  • Private accounts: every exam belongs to the teacher who created it, protected by real authentication.
